Privacy Policy

Challenge Arena is a quiz and brain-game service. The current web version stores profile name, score and streak locally in your browser/device using local storage. We do not currently require an account or collect that profile data on a server.

If future versions add accounts, analytics, advertising, payments or other third-party services, this policy will be updated before those features are enabled.

For privacy questions, contact the project owner through the contact method published with the service.
