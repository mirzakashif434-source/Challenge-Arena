Privacy Policy

Challenge Arena is a quiz and brain-game service. When you create an account, your email address and authentication information are handled by Supabase Authentication so you can sign in. Your optional display name is stored in the Challenge Arena profiles database.

When you play while signed in, Challenge Arena stores your game attempts, including category, score, correct answers, total questions and play time, in the Challenge Arena database. Your account data is protected with row-level security so signed-in users can access only their own profile and game attempts through the app.

The web version also uses local storage on your browser/device for game progress such as points, streak, best score and total correct answers. Signing in allows account-related profile and game-attempt data to be synced with the database.

Challenge Arena does not currently enable advertising, paid subscriptions or betting. If these features or other third-party services are added, this policy will be updated before they are enabled.

For privacy questions, contact the project owner through the contact method published with the service.
